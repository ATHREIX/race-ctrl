# Product

## Register

product

## Users

HYROX Singles Open and Pro athletes preparing for an upcoming race or reviewing a completed race. They use the product primarily on mobile around training and race day, and on laptops when exploring deeper predictions, comparisons, and performance analysis. Coaches and gym-management workflows are outside the initial scope.

## Product Purpose

Create a complete HYROX performance intelligence loop that turns training benchmarks and race results into a credible finish-time range, executable pacing plan, post-race diagnosis, and focused next improvement. Success means an athlete can quickly answer four questions: what am I capable of, how should I race, where did my time go, and what should I train next?

## Brand Personality

Precise, intense, elite. The product should feel like a performance instrument built for competition: direct, controlled, and physically charged. Its voice is concise and confident without pretending uncertain predictions are facts.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic SaaS admin dashboard, spreadsheet-heavy enterprise software, a clinical medical portal, a neon cyberpunk interface, or a gamified fitness app. Avoid glitter, ornamental effects, decorative animation, excessive cards, cheerful lifestyle imagery, and chatbot-first interaction. WHOOP-style performance clarity and HYROX competition energy are references, but the product must develop its own identity rather than imitate either brand.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the athlete's next decision, not the amount of available data.
2. Make uncertainty visible through ranges, confidence, and benchmark-quality signals.
3. Turn every diagnosis into a specific, achievable performance action.
4. Use motion to explain effort, sequence, fatigue, and time—not as decoration.
5. Preserve a fast race-day path while allowing deeper analysis on larger screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus states, semantic structure, and screen-reader-friendly labels. Never communicate status through color alone. Provide a reduced-motion experience that preserves meaning while replacing intense transitions and animated data sequences with restrained fades or immediate state changes.
