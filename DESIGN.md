# Design System: HYROX Performance Intelligence

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Race Control Room"**

The interface feels like an athlete has entered a private race-control environment: pitch black, exact, immediate, and built around the next performance decision. WHOOP supplies the reference for personal telemetry, HYROX supplies the physical competition energy, and Formula 1 timing graphics supply the reference for rapid split comparison and consequential motion.

The system is mobile-first and structurally expands for laptop analysis. Motion can be cinematic at moments of transition, prediction, and diagnosis, but every animation must reveal sequence, effort, fatigue, or time. It must never feel like generic business software, a neon cyberpunk game, or an entertainment overlay.

**Key Characteristics:**

- True-black environment with high-contrast white information
- Strong editorial hierarchy without ornamental display type
- Dense performance data revealed progressively
- Athletic, directional motion tied to race sequence
- Fast race-day scanning and deeper laptop exploration

### Named Rules

**The Monochrome Discipline Rule.** Black and white carry the interface. Saturated color is forbidden unless it communicates a real performance state.

**The No Decorative Glow Rule.** Light may trace an active metric or motion path, but glow never exists as background decoration.

**Display Font:** Single technical sans `[font family to be chosen at implementation]`

**Label/Mono Font:** Numeric companion or tabular-number style `[font treatment to be chosen at implementation]`

**Character:** Compact, engineered, and highly legible under physical stress. One family should carry the system, while tabular numerals and weight changes distinguish telemetry from explanation.

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** Condensed or tightly proportioned bold style for decisive race outcomes.
- **Headline:** Strong sentence-case hierarchy for analysis and prescribed actions.
- **Title:** Compact section and segment titles.
- **Body:** Calm explanatory copy capped at a readable measure.
- **Label:** Short, functional language with tabular numerals for times and deltas.

### Named Rules

**The Time Is Content Rule.** Times, paces, ranks, and deltas must align and remain instantly comparable.

## 4. Elevation

The system is flat by default and gains depth through tonal separation, clipping, scale, and motion rather than soft dashboard shadows. Cinematic sequences may layer data traces temporarily, but resting screens return to a controlled black plane.

### Named Rules

**The Grounded Surface Rule.** Shadows are forbidden at rest; hierarchy comes from contrast, spacing, and clear region boundaries.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** make the athlete's current prediction, next action, and largest opportunity immediately visible.
- **Do** use choreography for race sequence, prediction calculation, split comparison, and cause-to-effect analysis.
- **Do** design mobile first and reveal additional comparison depth on laptop screens.
- **Do** provide a reduced-motion mode that preserves every relationship and outcome.

### Don't:

- **Don't** resemble a generic SaaS admin dashboard or spreadsheet-heavy enterprise software.
- **Don't** resemble a clinical medical portal, neon cyberpunk interface, or gamified fitness app.
- **Don't** use glitter, ornamental effects, cheerful lifestyle imagery, or decorative animation.
- **Don't** imitate WHOOP, HYROX, or Formula 1 branding; translate their useful performance principles into an original system.
- **Don't** make the chatbot the primary interface.
